# Recover a Failed Dictation

You spoke, but the widget shows an error or nothing pasted. Your words are safe —
recover them in under 30 seconds.

## Step-by-step

1. **See the error** — note the widget state (red = failure, message in tooltip).
2. **Open Settings** — right-click the floating mic → **Settings...**.
3. **Go to History** — click the **History** tab.
4. **Search** — type a word you said into the search box; press **Refresh** if needed.
5. **Copy** — select the entry and press **Copy**, or **double-click** it to copy.
6. **Re-paste** — focus your target app and press `Ctrl+V`.

## Retry vs. reuse

- **Retry** (speak again) when: the transcript itself is wrong or empty
  (bad mic, wrong source language, background noise).
- **Reuse** (copy from History) when: the transcript looks right but the paste
  or translation step failed (network/gateway issue, no focused window).
  Just wait for the provider to recover, then copy and paste.
