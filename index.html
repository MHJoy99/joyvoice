❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is JoyVoice?

JoyVoice is a free, open-source Windows floating microphone app. Press F8, speak in any of 10 languages, and clean translated text is automatically pasted into whatever application currently has focus.

Is JoyVoice free?

Yes. JoyVoice is free and open-source under the MIT license. Cloud dictation uses your own API gateway key at roughly $0.001 per dictation, and the opt-in Free & Offline Mode needs no API key at all.

What languages does JoyVoice support?

10 languages for dictation and translation: Bangla, English, Russian, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese (Mandarin), Japanese, French, and Portuguese — with automatic source-language detection.

How is JoyVoice different from Windows voice typing (Win+H)?

Win+H covers fewer languages, needs manual language switching, and has no translation targets. JoyVoice auto-detects 10 languages, translates into any of the 10 target languages, pastes clipboard-safely with retry, and adds text styles, history, and global hotkeys.

Where do JoyVoice logs go?

Logs live at %APPDATA%\JoyVoice\joyvoice.log, alongside settings.json (settings) and history.json (dictation history). History is saved before every paste, so text is never lost.
